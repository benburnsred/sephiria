Sephiria HM60 Builds

Every build here is built around the HM60 difficulty and made to be consistent at that level. None of them requires rerolling a specific artifact on the 1st drop or on the floor 1 boss drop.

Required grab at all costs Top / High artifact priority tiers Out of combo worth taking outside your combos Dashed red combo target that most likely won't happen

Using presets: press Copy next to a build's preset code, then paste the whole code into Sephiria's preset import field to load that setup. Builds with more than one code are labelled by run (e.g. Harsh Trial), so pick the one that matches yours. If the game rejects a code, check nothing got cut off and that your save has the build's items unlocked.

Full notes

Put the Trainee Duelist's Epaulette artifact on top of the 1st line and stack as much Golden Needles underneath.

You're putting it on the far left because you want to upgrade all the compasses with either Justice, Simultaneity or Transference tablets, but also since bonus slot appears on the bottom left first.

I prefer starting with Trainee Duelist's Epaulette for easy early game parries but starting with Kriton's Seal is another possibility, as it helps getting a ton of Golden Needles.

Sturdy combo will be completed by stacking needles, so get Precision & Wind Song first.

The other level 3 upgrade DARK VEIL is another good contender since it has better homing and makes proccing last hit combos (Duelist's Epaulette) ten times easier.

Another strong candidate: The HARSH TRIAL dagger. It's by far the best dps output when using the Duelist's Epaulette, as the effect procs on every special attack.

Although, be aware that you'll have to farm the 50 parries before upgrading the weapon to level 3.

Wind Song wouldn't be needed in that case so just replace it with Shadow(50%) in the Fruit Skewer to help with the close combat. And replace the starting legendary artifact with the Rainbow Feather.

Full notes

Sacred Jar adds companion damage to its normal attack and Alfonso's Horn gives +80% at MAX level, it's the highest boost you could ever get from a 5★ starting artifact.

And since it's the only artifact giving this MUCH (Flux Coupler Mk.2 only gives +12% at MAX level), you can just ignore the Companion artifacts and focus on the three main combos normal attack builds play around: Precision ; Sturdy & Wind Song

Sun of the Archives is ESSENTIAL for this build to work, upgrade Academy & Solar Blade combos up to ±8 and use all dices on the 2nd floor boss drop until you found it (odds are boosted on boss drops).

Ilonica's Staff cast randoms bolts for 0 mp in quick succession, which makes it the best candidate for Sun of the Archives first effect which activates solar blade 2 additional times on grimoire damage.

Keep in mind that it ALSO increases solar blade dmg by 1% for each 1% of grimoire damage increased. (GOATED artifact)

The damage of this build only relies on solar blades, upgrading bolt spellbooks is useless. Jeweled Armor is also ineffective, don't take it.

Having at least 2 bolt spellbooks is also NECESSARY in order to keep casting indefinitely (with enough grimoire haste obviously). Getting a Mage's Coin will help find one early.

Full notes

Magic Blades aren't boosted by any elements, only MAX mp, grimoire damage & crit dmg matters. 4 combos are all you need. Negociation is good to have as a last combo, it boosts the overall dmg of 20% when holding 4000 coins.

The Empty Sword artifact is personal preference, but it allows you to have guarenteed final mp % as well as making the early game easy with the Ice Bolt spellbook thanks to its huge grimoire dmg boost (+30% at MAX level).

If you want more damage for the end game, you could go for 10 willpower and start with a Teabag.

But I'd advise not doing so because it would be too inconsistent, you would have to look for a random bolt spellbook drop and will end up having to restart by the start of the 2nd floor if none has been found.

Magic Blades are dealing 20 + 70% of the missing mp. So let's say you've got 100 out of 600 mp left, the blades will deal 370 dmg each ((0,7*500)+20=370).

Wing-Eared Rabbit is the best costume for running out of mana points since it starts with a +50% mp cost for self-buff spells.

The goal is to acquire a moderate amount of buff spells (3~4)* in order to quickly be low on mana points and get a huge damage boost.

And since the blades activate on grimoire use, you ABSOLUTELY want the Watering Can that activates the effect twice. *White Cape is great to have for that reason.
